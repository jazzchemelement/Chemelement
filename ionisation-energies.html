📚 Understanding Ionisation Energies

1st Ionisation Energy: Energy required to remove the outermost electron from a neutral atom.
2nd Ionisation Energy: Energy required to remove an electron from a 1+ ion.
Pattern: Notice how ionisation energy increases dramatically when removing electrons from inner shells (lower energy levels).
